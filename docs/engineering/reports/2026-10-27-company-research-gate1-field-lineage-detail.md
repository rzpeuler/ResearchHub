**Shared metadata rule:** period end aliases are `report_date` / `REPORT_DATE` / `reportDate`; publication aliases are `publication_date` / `NOTICE_DATE` / `publicationDate`. Flow basis is explicit only when `period_basis` is `QUARTER` or `YTD`; otherwise `YTD` may be inferred from `REPORT_DATE` or `operating_revenue`, else `UNKNOWN`. Balance/debt fields are forced to `POINT_IN_TIME`. CNY can be inferred for every metric simply because revenue exists on the row. Every normalized history observation is `pointInTimeSafe:false` and `pointInTimeStatus` is `CURRENT_VALUE_ONLY` or `UNVERIFIED`; retrieval date is not a historical revision timestamp.

## P0-required repairs versus deferred data quality

**Required for the P0 paths:** fail closed on missing period, source scope, unit/currency, publication boundary, numeric version, table label, or source conflict; preserve exact candidate and paragraph; use the currently verified filing EPS and current close for descriptive historical PE; report attempted provider/fallback and per-field gap; do not synthesize Scenario/Variant inputs.

**Deferred unless separately approved:** new public Common Data metric IDs/source policies, new providers or paid data, historical statement revision feeds, general per-metric conflict provenance across all fields, richer OHLCV currency/adjustment schema, and Knowledge schema changes. These require an explicit design and compatibility review before implementation.
