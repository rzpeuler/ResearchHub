# Company Research quality closure

The approved task preserves Data Layer v1, the flat Research Skill registry, Workflow-owned execution, the Report contract, and Knowledge Schema 0.4 / Storage 1. It replaces the single company-synthesis call with ten independently bounded research methods and a final report assembly operation. No Agent Runtime, provider registry, Skill hierarchy, or canonical write shortcut is introduced.

## Execution and interfaces

`company_research` selects the ten methods listed in `COMPANY_RESEARCH_METHOD_IDS`. Workflow loads each canonical SKILL.md, prepares attributed financial calculations, and supplies relevant full-document excerpts with stable original paragraph locators. Methods return exact owned section IDs, findings separated into fact/inference/assumption, evidence positions, limitations, and risk paths. Each method has at most two semantic attempts across the initial and supplemental acquisition rounds. Workflow may make two supplemental acquisition rounds within the shared ten-minute acquisition budget. Two independent methods run concurrently. Cancellation reaches both acquisition and reasoning, including Source Library wrappers.

Four new peer methods cover company history/strategy, management/governance, products/technology, and company risks. Existing deterministic financial-quality, business-driver, and capital-allocation helpers remain independently callable. Financial preparation rejects conflicting values and incompatible monetary units/currencies/bases. Market sizing first extracts source-bound operands, then validates and calculates them in code; no speculative or overlapping markets are silently aggregated.

Content-review failures return to the responsible atomic methods with the review reasons and targeted supplemental evidence. Already completed unrelated methods are retained. The two-attempt allowance is global per semantic operation, including formatting repair and subsequent acquisition rounds; final synthesis and content review each also have at most two calls. Exhaustion preserves the incomplete report, review snapshots, exact supplied evidence and visible output for each method attempt. Critique is guidance to recheck original sources, never new evidence. The independent reviewer must reject named competitors drawn only from paid report catalogs or generic market alternatives.

The final assembly retains all nineteen stable section IDs. Sixteen core sections must answer their research question and have attributable located evidence. Full publishable method analysis belongs in `sections`; the separate method markdown is a concise summary. Valuation, Bull/Base/Bear, and Variant Perception retain distinct missing-input/source/impact diagnostics in this first-coverage slice. The final synthesis cannot overwrite peer methods or insert unsupported numeric conclusions. Numeric grounding is a conservative novelty check, not a proof of semantic entailment. An independent Workflow semantic review therefore checks each core question, cited original paragraphs, numeric reproducibility, and company risk transmission before completion; it has at most two attempts and fails closed. The real-report acceptance review remains mandatory.

## Acquisition and provenance

Company-only discovery prioritizes complete Chinese annual reports and the latest interim report; unrelated recent corporate actions cannot exhaust the evidence budget. Structured financial history includes three-statement observations with explicit currency, unit, YTD/quarter/snapshot basis, and unresolved source conflicts. Tencent provides a market-data alternative. A conservative official financial extractor provides an S0 fallback when the structured source is unavailable; it requires exact labels, fiscal headers, and explicit units.

Public web search discovers candidates only. Selected bodies must be fetched and normalized through a Plugin. Bounded site-directory traversal and balanced topic selection cover company, industry/competition, and investor materials. Publication-unknown live pages may be cited as current report context, but are excluded from historical/canonical evidence eligibility; retrieval never becomes a fabricated publication timestamp. Source health advice requires repeated real source failures and a subsequently validated alternative before recommending replacement; it does not rewrite global policies.

The optional runtime candidate cache stores only metadata for previously fetched, normalized company-specific HTTPS URLs. Search failures can reuse those discovery candidates, but each execution must fetch and normalize the body again under the same URL, redirect and public-DNS checks. Cached timestamps and hashes are labeled as prior discovery metadata, not current evidence or publication dates. No body, search snippet or paid catalog substitutes for a substantive fetched article. Captcha and access challenges remain explicit source failures.

Born-digital PDF text extraction is an explicit company-client option inside the existing Docling Plugin. It preserves page blocks and original bytes, labels the absence of reconstructed tables, and falls back to the existing layout parser for scanned/insufficient text. Normal ingestion and Earnings retain their original layout path.

## Completion and storage

Source admissibility and research completion are independent gates. Degraded, unexecuted, unsupported, or empty core results cannot return `completed`. A blocked report and per-method diagnostics remain readable. Readonly reports retain external URLs and exact evidence positions even with no canonical Source/Claim IDs. Report quality/method/evidence-location metadata is optional and compatible with older reports.

The configured Pi model remains unchanged. Company JSON results may be serialized through one schema-constrained `submit_research_result` tool call; this is result transport only, executes no tool, and still validates locally. Other reasoning callers retain the legacy text path. Invalid outputs retain actionable schema diagnostics in runtime-data rather than silently substituting a completed baseline.

Only passing research submits eligible proposals via Knowledge Production Gateway, validation, ChangeSet, and Writer. Explicit `writeKnowledge=true` may establish the verified external Company through that same path; readonly first coverage never creates it. Report subject refs attach only persisted entities. Production KBs remain readonly during acceptance; a disposable KB verifies authorized persistence and reload. Runtime artifacts live under runtime-data, outside source-controlled Knowledge. Shared account/quota failures stop further semantic operations and supplemental acquisition, preserve completed results, and mark uninvoked methods with zero attempts.
