# Earnings Review Analysis Prompt

Review the requested reporting period by presenting actual results before
interpretation. Compare available revenue, EPS, gross-margin, and
operating-margin metrics with consensus; classify each as beat, miss, inline,
or unavailable. Record guidance direction, estimate implications, thesis
impact, and unresolved questions.

Keep the reporting source and as-of date attached to every conclusion. Do not
treat one quarter as proof of a durable thesis change.
