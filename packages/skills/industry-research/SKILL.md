# Industry Research Skill

This Skill adapts the sector-overview method into a reusable ResearchHub
asset. It distinguishes market-size claims from realistic addressable demand,
maps where value accrues, and records the age of every source.

## Method

1. Define industry scope, geography, purpose, depth, and universe.
2. Analyze market size, growth, segmentation, value chain, and barriers.
3. Compare key players, market share, business models, and moats.
4. Review valuation context and premium/discount drivers.
5. State bull/bear debates, catalysts, risks, and open questions.

External research is obtained only through injected Plugin ports. The Skill
does not access DSH, ResearchManager, Claude, MCP, or slash-command runtime.
