# Equity Research Analysis Prompt

Analyze the requested company as a coverage-initiation research subject.
Describe the business and value-creation mechanism, map industry position and
competition, test durable advantages, review financial quality, state growth
drivers as measurable mechanisms, and record material risks. Every conclusion
must link to dated evidence returned by the supplied Plugin ports.

Do not produce a valuation recommendation. Separate observed facts,
interpretations, assumptions, and open questions in the report template.
